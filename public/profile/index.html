Profile

Janorovic Volkov is the pen name of Radit Jibril Irawan, an Indonesian software developer, writer, and Wikimedia contributor. He has contributed to the Indonesian Wikipedia since 2021 and became a Wikipedia administrator in 2025. He is a member of Wikimedia Indonesia and an interface administrator, with a particular interest in building tools, gadgets, bots, and technical infrastructure for Wikimedia projects.

His interests include programming, psychology, politics, philosophy, and Central European history and culture, especially Czechia and Slovakia. He is interested not only in creating content, but also in the technical and organizational systems that support collaborative knowledge.

Software projects

Outside Wikimedia, Janorovic develops Liska Linux, an independent Linux distribution guided by the principle:

“Precision over chaos. Isolation over compromise.”

Liska Linux emphasizes user control, minimalism, and explicit system management. Its installation and configuration approach is similar to Arch Linux, while its package-management philosophy takes inspiration from Gentoo. The distribution has its own Rust-based package manager, LKPM (Liska Package Manager). LKPM does not install dependencies automatically by default, giving users direct control over that process.

The Liska Linux ecosystem also includes LKFS for filesystem formatting and swap management, LiskaISO for creating installation media, and LkSystem, a Rust-based Linux init and service supervision system in development. Janorovic also builds reusable Rust libraries and utilities to share a common foundation across the ecosystem.

Black Fox is a compact recovery-focused operating system for repairing, inspecting, and recovering other systems. It combines a minimal Linux kernel and BusyBox-based recovery environment with a lightweight Rust init system. Its toolkit includes filesystem and partition utilities, disk and image recovery tools, disk-health monitoring, RAID utilities, file synchronization and recovery tools, and other maintenance and diagnostic software. It also includes an EFI Shell environment, memory-testing capabilities, and QEMU-based testing workflows.

Through Liska Linux and Black Fox, Janorovic explores operating-system development, system recovery, low-level Linux tools, package management, and software infrastructure. Across his Wikimedia and software work, he is interested in building reliable systems for collaborative knowledge, operating systems, and developer tools.

Education and background

Janorovic is studying software engineering at Anwar Medika University. He previously studied mechanical engineering at a vocational high school and is interested in the connections between technology, philosophy, and history.

He tends to look at problems as systems, paying attention to how their parts interact. This perspective informs both his software projects and his interest in mechanical systems.
